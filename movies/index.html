Four films that stay with me.

  • 12 Angry Men

    It is a masterclass in tension, dialogue, and conviction with almost no wasted scene.

  • The Iron Claw

    The family dynamic is raw and devastating, and it stayed with me long after watching.

  • About Time

    It makes ordinary moments feel meaningful and is one of the warmest films I know.

  • Interstellar

    I love how it pairs big scientific ideas with a deeply human emotional core.
