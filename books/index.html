Four books I keep returning to.

  • The Old Man and the Sea

    It is simple on the surface but says so much about discipline, pride, and endurance.

  • The Brothers Karamazov

    It goes deep on morality, faith, and responsibility without ever feeling abstract.

  • The Kite Runner

    The emotional arc around guilt, loyalty, and redemption always hits hard for me.

  • On Writing: A Memoir of the Craft

    It is practical, honest, and one of the best books I have read on creative work.
